# About

I am Alexander Wolf, co-founder and CTO of [8mylez](https://8mylez.com), a Shopware agency.
Usually `myl3z` online, and [xndrdev](https://github.com/xndrdev) on GitHub.

## This site

Not a portfolio — a notebook that happens to be public.

The motive is self-serving: writing something down means understanding it better, and having
to explain it reveals that you did not understand it as well as you thought. While writing
the Pi-hole notes it struck me that the server was the only device in the house not using its
own filter. That does not stand out while configuring. It does while phrasing.

I am less interested in what works right now than in why it works, and what happens when it
stops. Which is why the mistakes are here too: a note that only shows the finished state
leaves out the part where you actually got stuck.

## In my spare time

Right now mostly a **homelab**, built one step at a time — one device, one service, write it
down, carry on. It started with a thin client and a DNS server of my own; next comes a
network with proper segments, and after that a hypervisor. It is less about the services than
about having seen the layers from below once, instead of renting them.

Alongside that, **cyber security**: labs, tooling, reading notes. Plus an Arch Linux setup
that never quite reaches finished, which is presumably the point of it.

The line between work and evenings is admittedly thin in all of this.
